# Methodology and Assumptions
## BORI (Kurnur) Dam – Breach Analysis

---

## 1. General Methodology
The breach analysis is based on time-series hydrograph data generated from breach and controlled release simulations. The methodology focuses on interpreting hydraulic response rather than numerical breach modeling.

Steps followed:
1. Import breach hydrograph data from Excel.
2. Align time stamps across all parameters.
3. Identify peak discharge and critical breach time.
4. Analyze water level drawdown trends.
5. Evaluate breach widening and velocity evolution (where available).
6. Present results through engineering-grade plots.

---

## 2. Piping Failure Scenario
- Breach initiation assumed within embankment.
- Gradual increase in discharge until peak.
- Breach width and velocity evolve progressively.
- No sediment transport considered.

---

## 3. Overtopping Failure Scenario
- Breach initiation due to crest overtopping.
- Rapid rise in discharge.
- Sudden enlargement of breach width.
- Faster drawdown compared to piping failure.

---

## 4. Large Controlled Release Scenario
- No breach formation assumed.
- Discharge governed by gate operation.
- Headwater and tailwater elevations respond smoothly.
- Used as a reference operational scenario.

---

## 5. Assumptions
- Reservoir inflow during breach is negligible.
- Tailwater effects are based on provided data.
- No downstream channel routing included.
- Structural behavior simplified to hydraulic response only.
- Velocity data unavailable for controlled release case.

---

## 6. Limitations
- No 2D inundation modeling.
- Breach parameters are not physically simulated.
- Results should be interpreted as **indicative**, not final design values.

---

## 7. Intended Use
- Academic demonstration
- Preliminary engineering understanding
- Report annexures and visualization
